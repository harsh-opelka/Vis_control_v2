C. Does on_row_tripwire / on_row_cleared depend on on_row_confirmed state?
-------------------------------------------------------------------
No. Read viscontrol/core/training_data_collector.py in full:
  - `_mark_if_new(row_id, stage)` (lines 235-251) tracks
    `self._captured_stages: dict[int, set[str]]` — a per-row_id SET of
    stage strings already captured. Each stage ("confirmed", "tripwire",
    "cleared") is added/checked independently; there is no ordering
    requirement, no cross-stage state, and no field on `RowCaptureEvent`
    or `RowPieceData` that "confirmed" populates for later stages to
    read. `on_row_tripwire`/`on_row_cleared` build their own
    `RowCaptureEvent` fresh from the `piece_data`/`outcome` arguments
    passed in by the CALLER (MainWindow) each time — nothing is threaded
    through from a prior `on_row_confirmed` call inside the collector.
  - The only shared structure touched by all three is the eviction deque
    (`self._row_id_eviction_order`), which only cares about row_id
    (first-seen-wins for eviction ordering), never about which specific
    stage was seen first. Removing "confirmed" from `_STAGES` and
    deleting `on_row_confirmed` leaves `_mark_if_new` fully generic
    (it never hardcodes stage names) — no change needed there beyond
    the docstring/`_STAGES` tuple.
  - On the MainWindow side, `_capture_cloth_image()` and
    `_row_piece_data(row)` (main_window.py:1882-1908) are stateless
    helpers re-derived fresh on every call from `self._latest_frame` /
    the `row` argument — `on_row_tripwire_capture`/`on_row_terminal_capture`
    call them independently and do not read anything
    `_on_row_confirmed_capture` would have set.

CONCLUSION: removing `on_row_confirmed` (both the collector method and
the MainWindow call site/closure) is a clean, fully isolated deletion —
`on_row_tripwire` and `on_row_cleared` need zero changes to keep working
exactly as before, and the existing "every row gets at least 'cleared'"
guarantee is untouched (it never depended on "confirmed" firing first).
