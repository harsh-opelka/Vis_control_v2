B. IS 30px ENOUGH SEPARATION FOR A ~174px-RADIUS PIECE?
------------------------------------------------------------
No — nowhere close.

`minDist` in `cv2.HoughCircles` is measured in PIXELS between CIRCLE
CENTERS, not a fraction of anything. For two circles not to be treated as
the same detection, `minDist` needs to be on the order of the circle's own
size (typically ~0.8-1.5x the diameter) — otherwise Hough's accumulator
keeps finding new local maxima just a few pixels apart on the same real
edge (concentric/near-concentric "duplicate" circles around one true
piece), because a strong circular edge produces a broad, smoothly-varying
peak in Hough space, not a single point.

With a measured piece radius of ~174 px (diameter ~348 px), a `minDist` of
30 px is roughly:

    30 / 348 ≈ 8.6% of the piece's own diameter

i.e. up to ~11 duplicate "centers" could be packed across a single piece's
diameter and still all satisfy the 30px separation constraint. This
directly explains "853 pieces per frame" on a cloth with roughly 8-24 real
pieces: 853 / ~20 real pieces ≈ 40+ spurious duplicate circles reported per
real piece — consistent with a minDist an order of magnitude too small for
this piece size.

The root cause is structural, not a bad constant: `sweep_min_radius_px`
(20 px, the SWEEP FLOOR) has no relationship to the ACTUAL piece radius
(~174 px here) — it's deliberately wide-open so pieces of any size in the
0.5x-2x range can be found at all. Deriving `minDist` from the sweep floor
(`sweep_min_radius_px * 1.5 = 30px`) therefore only ever produces a
correctly-sized `minDist` by coincidence, when the real piece happens to be
close to the sweep floor. For any piece meaningfully larger than
`sweep_min_radius_px` (which is the common case — `sweep_min_radius_px` is
a fixed conservative floor, not tuned per profile), the substitution
under-constrains `minDist` and duplicate/concentric circles flood the
result. This is fixed in size_calibration.py via the two-pass approach
(pass 1 at the loose sweep-floor `minDist` to get a rough radius estimate
that is BY DESIGN robust to duplicates via the median, then pass 2 at
`minDist = rough_median_radius * 1.3` — scaled to the piece the sweep
actually found, not the sweep's search floor). See calibration_bugfix_result.txt.
