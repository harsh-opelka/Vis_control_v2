THE TWO-PASS FIX AND WHAT minDist IT PRODUCES AT 174px RADIUS
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Old (buggy) behavior: a single Hough pass with
`minDist = sweep_min_radius_px * 1.5 = 20 * 1.5 = 30.0 px`, unrelated to
the actual piece size. On a ~174px-radius piece this is ~9% of the piece's
own diameter — Hough's accumulator finds dozens of near-duplicate local
maxima on the same real circular edge, producing the observed
"853 pieces per frame" on a cloth with ~8-24 real pieces.

New behavior (SizeCalibrator.add_frame):

  Pass 1 — loose, `minDist = sweep_min_radius_px` (20.0 px in the default
  config; still duplicate-prone, but only used to get a ROUGH radius
  estimate, and the median is robust to duplicate clusters).

  rough_median_radius = statistics.median(pass-1 radii)

  Pass 2 — re-run on the SAME frame with
  `minDist = max(1.0, rough_median_radius * 1.3)`.

At the real ~174px radius from the failing run:

  rough_median_radius ≈ 174.0
  minDist (pass 2)     = max(1.0, 174.0 * 1.3) = 226.2 px

226.2 px is ~65% of the piece's 348px diameter — comfortably in the
"roughly one piece diameter" range needed to suppress duplicate/concentric
detections on a single real piece, and two full orders of magnitude larger
than the old fixed 30px. Verified live in a smoke test with 15 real
well-separated ~90px-radius circles on a synthetic frame: pass 1 found 19
detections (a few duplicate edge hits), rough_median_radius=90.4,
pass 2 (minDist=117.6=90.4*1.3) collapsed this to exactly 15 — the true
piece count. Every frame logged both passes:

  [CAL] CAL-SWEEP-PASS1 frame=29 raw_detections=19 rough_median_radius=90.4
  [CAL] CAL-SWEEP-PASS2 frame=29 min_dist_used=117.6 final_detections=15

The `max_plausible_pieces_per_frame` sanity cap (default 40) additionally
guards against a pathological frame where even pass 2's scaled minDist
isn't enough (e.g. reflections/noise dominating pass 1's rough radius
estimate) — that frame is discarded entirely (not stored, not counted
toward `frames_used`) and logged as
`[CAL] CAL-FRAME-REJECTED frame={} reason=implausible_count count={}`,
verified by test_k10_duplicate_circle_rejection_frame_discarded.

APPLY WRITES EXACTLY TWO FIELDS
------------------------------------
`MainWindow._on_size_calibration_apply` (viscontrol/ui/main_window.py)
assigns to exactly:

  detection.hough.min_radius_px   <- result.suggested_min_radius_px
  detection.hough.max_radius_px   <- result.suggested_max_radius_px

and nothing else — no `proximity_clustering.tolerance_px` write (removed),
no profile `expected_area_px`/`expected_width_px`/`expected_height_px`
write. Per the answer to STEP 6's question (asked and answered before this
change was made: "leave profile fields unchanged"), the handler instead
logs a one-time note after the two CAL-APPLIED lines:

  [CAL] CAL-APPLIED field=detection.hough.min_radius_px old=<old> new=<new>
  [CAL] CAL-APPLIED field=detection.hough.max_radius_px old=<old> new=<new>
  [CAL] profile expected_area/width/height were not modified; update manually if needed.

The write still goes through the exact same `self._save_cfg()` mechanism
every other wizard-page setting uses (immediate write outside the wizard,
deferred-to-Finish while the wizard is open, discarded on Cancel — see
calibration_inspection.txt part D).
