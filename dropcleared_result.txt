CONFIRMATION: NO ORPHANED DEDUP/SAMPLING LOGIC LEFT OVER
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Per dropcleared_inspection.txt: the "both stages must agree" cache
(`self._row_sample_decisions` + `self._row_sample_eviction_order`)
existed for exactly one reason — letting two independent call paths
(tripwire and cleared) agree on one save/skip decision for the same
row_id without double-incrementing `_rows_seen_count`. With
`on_row_cleared` gone, `on_row_tripwire` is the only entry point into
`_capture()`, and `_mark_if_new` (checked first, before the sampling
filter is ever reached) already guarantees a given `(row_id, "tripwire")`
pair can never legitimately reach the sampling check twice. So:
  - `self._row_sample_decisions` and `self._row_sample_eviction_order`
    (both the `__init__` state and their only reader/writer,
    `_should_capture_row`) were deleted entirely — not just left unused.
  - `_should_capture_row` was simplified from a row_id-keyed
    cache-or-compute method to a plain, unconditional
    increment-and-modulo check (`row_id` parameter removed from its
    signature too, since nothing in the simplified body needs it — its
    one call site in `_capture()` updated to match).
  - `RowCaptureEvent.outcome` removed (see above) — it existed solely to
    carry `on_row_cleared`'s outcome argument through to the sidecar;
    with that method gone it would have been permanently `None` forever,
    which is dead schema, not "kept for future stability" the way
    `fused` legitimately still is (fused's docstring explains real,
    ongoing unavailability; outcome's reason for being None would have
    been "the caller that set it no longer exists").
  - `_mark_if_new`/`self._captured_stages`/`self._row_id_eviction_order`
    were left completely untouched — confirmed these are a SEPARATE
    concern (per-stage duplicate-call protection, still fully needed
    with one stage) from the removed "both stages agree" sampling cache,
    per dropcleared_inspection.txt's analysis. Not part of what this
    task's constraint meant by "dead code from the removed both-stages
    logic."
  - `grep -n "_row_sample_decisions\|_row_sample_eviction_order\|on_row_cleared"
    viscontrol/core/training_data_collector.py` (post-change): zero
    hits anywhere in the file.
